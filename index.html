How to Play Buraco

1. Objective

Form combinations of cards, complete Buracos, collect points, and reach 2,100 points before your opponent.

2. Setup

The game uses two standard 52-card decks and four printed jokers. Players receive initial hands, and two separate 11-card stashes are prepared.

3. Turn Sequence

  1. Draw from the draw pile or take the discard pile.
  2. Create new melds or extend existing melds.
  3. Discard one card to finish your turn.

4. Drawing Cards

A player can draw from the draw pile or take the discard pile according to the implemented discard-pile rules.

5. Creating Sequences and Sets

A meld is a valid combination of cards. Sequences consist of consecutive cards of the same suit. Sets consist of cards of the same rank, if enabled by the selected rules.

6. Wild Cards

Jokers and twos can act as wild cards. Their permitted use and restrictions depend on the selected rules.

7. Completing a Buraco

A Buraco is a meld containing at least seven cards. Clean Buracos contain no wild cards; dirty Buracos contain at least one wild card.

8. Card Values

Cards have individual point values. Jokers, twos, face cards, aces, and numbered cards may have different values.

9. Stash Mechanics

Each player has an 11-card stash. When a player empties their hand for the first time, they may take their stash. The stash contents remain private.

10. Round-End Scoring

Points are calculated from melds, completed Buracos, remaining cards, and applicable bonuses or penalties.

11. Game Settings and Assumptions

Some Buraco rules vary by region and variant. The following implementation defaults are used unless explicitly configured otherwise.
